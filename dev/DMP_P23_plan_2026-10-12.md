## Guardrails

- Keep P23 test integration isolated from production API/schema changes unless a concrete contract requirement demands a change; obtain owner direction for any material wire/security/compatibility choice.
- Do not treat two Python peer instances, same-implementation round trips, fixtures, or a scripted trace as independent interoperability.
- Do not duplicate protocol behavior in the orchestrator. Route only serialized endpoint frames through transport adapters; endpoint acceptance and terminal results must come from the C and Python endpoint implementations.
- Keep deterministic entropy/keys test-only. Default logs must not expose PSKs, private keys, plaintext payloads or handshake transcripts.
- Report host, simulation, MCU/runtime/RAM and physical evidence separately. No P23 result upgrades a physical-binding claim.
